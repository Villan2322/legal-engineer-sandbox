RETAIL LEASE AGREEMENT

Landlord: Coastal Retail Holdings, LP. Tenant: Bright Leaf Coffee Roasters,
LLC. Premises: 220 Harbor Street, Unit 4, Portland, Oregon.

Base Rent. Tenant agrees to pay Landlord monthly Base Rent in the amount of
$12,250.00, due and payable in advance on the first day of each month
during the Term, without prior demand.

Permitted Use. Tenant shall use the Premises exclusively for the retail
sale of coffee, tea, and related beverages and light food items, and for
no other use whatsoever.

Term of Lease. The Term of this Lease shall be eighty-four (84) months,
commencing on the Commencement Date set forth in the Basic Lease
Information and expiring, unless earlier terminated, on the last day of
the eighty-fourth full calendar month thereafter.

No Renewal Rights. Tenant acknowledges and agrees that Tenant has no
option, right, or privilege to renew or extend this Lease beyond the
expiration of the Term, and Landlord shall be under no obligation to offer
Tenant a new lease of the Premises.

Restrictions on Transfer. Tenant shall not, whether voluntarily or by
operation of law, assign, mortgage, pledge, encumber, or otherwise
transfer this Lease, nor sublet the Premises or any part thereof. Any
attempted assignment or subletting in violation of this Section shall be
void and shall, at Landlord's election, constitute a material default
under this Lease. This prohibition applies regardless of whether
Landlord's consent is sought.

Default; Notice and Cure. Should Tenant fail to perform any covenant or
obligation under this Lease, Landlord shall provide Tenant with written
notice specifying the nature of the default, and Tenant shall have ten
(10) days following delivery of such notice to cure the default in full.

General Provisions. Time is of the essence with respect to every provision
of this Lease. This Lease shall be binding upon and inure to the benefit
of the parties' respective successors and permitted assigns.
