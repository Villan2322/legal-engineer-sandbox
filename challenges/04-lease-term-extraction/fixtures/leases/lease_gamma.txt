INDUSTRIAL LEASE

This Lease is made between Ironwood Logistics Partners ("Landlord") and
Falcon Freight Systems, Inc. ("Tenant") for the warehouse facility located
at 900 Commerce Parkway, Building C, Memphis, Tennessee.

Rent. The monthly Base Rent payable by Tenant to Landlord shall be
$6,100.50, due on the first business day of each month for the duration
of the Term.

Term. This Lease shall run for a Term of thirty-six (36) months beginning
on the Commencement Date and ending thirty-six months thereafter, unless
earlier terminated pursuant to the terms hereof.

Use of Premises. Tenant shall use the Premises for warehousing,
distribution, and light assembly of consumer goods.

Renewal. This Lease contains no option to extend or renew the Term, and
any continued occupancy after expiration shall be a month-to-month
tenancy terminable at will, with Landlord under no obligation whatsoever
to grant Tenant a new lease or further term of any kind.

Assignment and Subletting. Tenant may freely assign this Lease or sublet
the Premises, in whole or in part, without obtaining Landlord's prior
consent, provided Tenant delivers written notice of any such assignment
or sublease to Landlord within ten (10) days thereafter.

Notice of Default. In the event of any default by Tenant under this
Lease, Landlord shall deliver written notice thereof, and Tenant shall
have thirty (30) days after such notice within which to cure the default,
failing which Landlord may pursue any remedy available under this Lease
or applicable law.

Entire Agreement. This document, together with any exhibits attached
hereto, constitutes the entire understanding between Landlord and Tenant
regarding the Premises.
