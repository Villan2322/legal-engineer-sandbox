OFFICE LEASE SUMMARY

This Lease is entered into between Prairie Fields Realty, LLC ("Landlord")
and Anchor & Vale Design Studio ("Tenant") for premises located at 55
Riverside Row, Suite 210, Des Moines, Iowa.

Base Rent. Tenant shall pay Base Rent of $4,750.00 per month, payable in
advance on the first day of each month throughout the Term, without any
deduction, setoff, or prior demand by Landlord.

Term. The Term of this Lease shall be twenty-four (24) months, commencing
on the Commencement Date and expiring twenty-four months thereafter,
unless earlier terminated as provided herein.

Permitted Use. Tenant shall use the Premises solely for interior design
studio and showroom purposes, and for no other purpose whatsoever.

Renewal Option. Tenant shall have one (1) option to renew this Lease for
an additional term of twelve (12) months, provided Tenant delivers
written notice of its intent to exercise such option not less than
ninety days prior to the expiration of the initial Term.

Assignment and Subletting. Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet
the Premises without the prior written consent of Landlord, which consent
shall not be unreasonably withheld.

Insurance. Tenant shall maintain commercial general liability insurance
in an amount not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence, naming Landlord as
an additional insured, throughout the Term of this Lease.

Parking and Common Areas. Tenant and its employees shall have the
non-exclusive right to use up to four unreserved parking spaces in the
building's surface lot, subject to such reasonable rules as Landlord may
adopt from time to time. Landlord shall maintain the common lobby,
corridors, and restrooms serving the Premises in good repair throughout
the Term, and Tenant shall reimburse Landlord for its proportionate share
of common area maintenance costs as set forth in a separate schedule.

Entire Agreement. This Lease represents the entire agreement of the
parties and may not be modified except in a writing signed by both
Landlord and Tenant.
